About Zuri Logistics Group

Reliable Transport, Logistics & Sourcing Solutions

Zuri Logistics Group (Pty) Ltd is a Johannesburg-based logistics and sourcing company serving customers across South Africa and the SADC region.

We support businesses and individuals with transport, freight forwarding, customs clearance, warehousing, removals, sourcing and related logistics services. We also assist customers with sourcing products and equipment across industries including agriculture, mining, engineering, technology, medical supplies and business equipment.

National Coverage

We provide logistics support throughout South Africa and the wider SADC region.

Reliable Service

Our focus is safe, efficient delivery, clear communication and practical logistics solutions.
