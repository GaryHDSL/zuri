About Zuri Logistics Group

Practical Logistics Solutions. Dependable Business Support.

Zuri Logistics Group (Pty) Ltd is a Johannesburg-based logistics and sourcing company supporting businesses and individuals with transport, freight, supply-chain and technical service requirements.

We work to connect customers with dependable logistics solutions, responsive service providers and practical support for operations across South Africa and the wider SADC region.
