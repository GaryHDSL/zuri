About Zuri Logistics

Fast, Reliable Transport and Logistics Solutions

Zuri Logistics Group (Pty) Ltd is a Johannesburg-based logistics and sourcing company serving customers across South Africa and the SADC region. We provide transport, freight forwarding, customs clearance, warehousing, removals, sourcing and related logistics services for businesses and individuals.
