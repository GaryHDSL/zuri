Price sources and methodology

LME prices shown are cash bid/ask midpoints calculated from the London Metal Exchange official prices table for 4 November 2026. They are exchange reference prices, not DRC export prices. Prices can move during the trading day.

For DRC export reference bulletins, consult the DRC Ministry of Foreign Trade – Mercuriales. For the exchange snapshot, consult LME Official Prices. Some products, including lithium, gold and tantalum, are not quoted here because a comparable current benchmark and unit were not verified for this snapshot.

PROCUREMENT SUPPORT

What we help buyers and suppliers coordinate.

A clear sourcing brief helps us understand the requirement and identify the right next steps.

Supplier identification

Capture product, origin, producer, available quantity and supplier documentation for review.

Specification & assay

Record grade, purity, moisture, impurities, assay certificates and inspection requirements.

Commercial coordination

Discuss indicative price basis, quantity, delivery terms, payment conditions and contract steps.

Transport coordination

Discuss route, loading point, destination, border requirements and cargo handling.

Due diligence

Encourage independent verification of supplier identity, title, permits, product and counterparties.

Buyer & seller enquiries

Submit your requirement or supply availability for review and possible introduction.
